# Demonstration

Two recorded runs of Stillwatch 0.5.0, edited into one video. Camera footage and captured event panels play at their original speed. Short title cards separate the sources. Source audio is omitted.

- **Suspected fall:** a staged EDF sequence. The system retains the episode, observes its development and leaves `UNKNOWN` when recovery is not established. This is not a confirmed injury.
- **Fire and smoke:** a FIRESENSE test sequence. The real cloud assessment reaches `URGENT`, requesting immediate human review.

These are selected demonstration clips, not an independent accuracy benchmark. No statuses were manually substituted. Both routes use the released CPU models and the real OpenAI decision provider. Text above the recordings explains the observed states; it is part of the edit.
